# Add your family line to the tree

Help us place you and your family correctly. Just write down the line of people
**from the top ancestor down to you** — each person, who they married, and their
children. You don't need to know anything technical. Copy the blank template at
the bottom, fill it in, and send it back.

**How to read the tree below:** each person sits one step to the **right** of their
parent. "m." means "married to".

## Example

```
Muhammad Shariff Hajiar (Pul-la-Kutty)      m. Ummu Zabila
└─ Muhammad Fawzy Shariff                    m. Ummu Faleela Abdul Majeed
   └─ Muhammad Yehiya Shariff                m. Fathima Fareeka
      ├─ Fathima Ameena Yehiya
      ├─ Fazal Ahamed Yehiya                 m. Fathima Hafsa Rasheed
      │  ├─ Ahamed Fadhil Fazal
      │  ├─ Hudha Aleeza Fazal
      │  └─ Hud Mikail Fazal
      └─ Fathima Azmiya Yehiya               m. Mohamed Cassim Farook
         ├─ Areeb Afzal Cassim
         └─ Adeel Ahsan Cassim
```

In this example, the lines down to Muhammad Yehiya Shariff show the top ancestor,
his son, and grandson. Underneath Muhammad Yehiya Shariff are his **three children**
(Fathima Ameena, Fazal Ahamed, and Fathima Azmiya Yehiya) — they are brothers and
sisters, so they all sit at the **same step** to the right. Each of them then has
their own spouse and children listed below them. Add your brothers and sisters and
their families the same way.

## Blank template (copy this and fill it in)

```
[Top ancestor]               m. [their spouse]
└─ [their child]             m. [their spouse]
   └─ [their child]          m. [their spouse]
      ├─ [you]               m. [your spouse]
      │  ├─ [your child]
      │  └─ [your child]
      └─ [your brother/sister]   m. [their spouse]
         ├─ [their child]
         └─ [their child]
```

## Tips

- Start from the oldest ancestor you know and work down, one generation per line.
- Move each new generation one step further to the right.
- Add as many or as few generations as you know — even a partial line helps.
- Brothers and sisters sit at the same step (the same distance to the right), each
  with their own spouse and children listed below them.
- List every spouse you can. If someone married more than once, add a line for each
  marriage and put each spouse's children under the right marriage.
- It's fine to write "not known" where you're unsure of a name.
