# Add your family line to the tree

Help us place you and your family correctly. Just write down the line of people
**from the top ancestor down to you** — each person, who they married, and their
children. You don't need to know anything technical. Copy the blank template at
the bottom, fill it in, and send it back.

Each person gets a short **code** like `a.`, `a.a.`, `a.a.b.` that shows who they
belong to. `m.` means "married to".

## How the numbering works

- The oldest ancestor is **`a.`**
- Each of a person's children adds one more letter to that person's code:
  `a.`'s children are **`a.a.`**, **`a.b.`**, **`a.c.`** …
- Keep going deeper the same way: `a.a.`'s children are **`a.a.a.`**, **`a.a.b.`** …
- Read the code like an address — it tells you exactly who the parent is.
  For example **`a.a.a.b.`** is the 2nd child of **`a.a.a.`**, and **`a.a.a.b.a.`**
  is the 1st child of **`a.a.a.b.`**
- Brothers and sisters share the same code except the **last letter**
  (e.g. `a.a.a.a.`, `a.a.a.b.`, `a.a.a.c.` are three siblings).

Each line is also indented a little to make it easier to read. The indentation is
only a visual aid — the **code** is what defines the relationship, so it's fine if
the spacing isn't perfectly lined up.

## Example

```
a.  Muhammad Shariff Hajiar (Pul-la-Kutty)  m. Ummu Zabila
  a.a.  Muhammad Fawzy Shariff  m. Ummu Faleela Abdul Majeed
    a.a.a.  Muhammad Yehiya Shariff  m. Fathima Fareeka
      a.a.a.a.  Fathima Ameena Yehiya
      a.a.a.b.  Fazal Ahamed Yehiya  m. Fathima Hafsa Rasheed
        a.a.a.b.a.  Ahamed Fadhil Fazal
        a.a.a.b.b.  Hudha Aleeza Fazal
        a.a.a.b.c.  Hud Mikail Fazal
      a.a.a.c.  Fathima Azmiya Yehiya  m. Mohamed Cassim Farook
        a.a.a.c.a.  Areeb Afzal Cassim
        a.a.a.c.b.  Adeel Ahsan Cassim
```

In this example, `a.a.a.` (Muhammad Yehiya Shariff) has **three children** —
`a.a.a.a.`, `a.a.a.b.` and `a.a.a.c.` — who are brothers and sisters. Fazal Ahamed
Yehiya (`a.a.a.b.`) then has three children of his own (`a.a.a.b.a.` to
`a.a.a.b.c.`). Add your own brothers, sisters, and their families the same way.

## Blank template (copy this and fill it in)

```
a.  [Top ancestor]  m. [their spouse]
  a.a.  [their child]  m. [their spouse]
    a.a.a.  [their child]  m. [their spouse]
      a.a.a.a.  [you]  m. [your spouse]
        a.a.a.a.a.  [your child]
        a.a.a.a.b.  [your child]
      a.a.a.b.  [your brother/sister]  m. [their spouse]
        a.a.a.b.a.  [their child]
        a.a.a.b.b.  [their child]
```

## Tips

- Start from the oldest ancestor you know and give them the code `a.`
- Give each child the parent's code plus a new letter; keep going deeper the same way.
- Indent each generation a little further to the right — it just makes the list
  easier to read.
- Add as many or as few generations as you know — even a partial line helps.
- List every spouse you can. If someone married more than once, add a line for each
  marriage and give that marriage's children codes under the right parent.
- It's fine to write "not known" where you're unsure of a name.
